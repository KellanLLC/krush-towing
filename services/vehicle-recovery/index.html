The job behind the photograph.

Recovery is about getting a vehicle onto the truck when it cannot help. A car with a seized brake or a locked steering wheel cannot be rolled; a car wedged between two others cannot be reached straight on. Those are solved with the wheel-lift arms, the winch and the bed, and with some patience.

On the feed it looks like the shop jobs: the truck backed into a bay in Queens with the arms down, cars brought in and taken out for repair, collision and auction runs.

When you call, say what is wrong and what is around it. "Will not go into neutral" and "parked tight on both ends" change which truck comes.
